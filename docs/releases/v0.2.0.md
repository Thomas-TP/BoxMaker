# Boxmaker v0.2.0 — Integrated latch and lighter box

**Unpublished build.** v0.2.0 was superseded by v0.2.1 before distribution. [Download the current Boxmaker release](https://github.com/Thomas-TP/BoxMaker/releases/tag/v0.8.3).

The sliding lid locks with an integrated rear tab: press its textured area and pull to open it. The earlier separate key and heavy reinforcement are removed from the new model. It has two parts, rounded corners, thinner panels, and local ribs. Across three test formats, estimated PLA use fell by 32–33% against standard v0.1 settings with the same useful cavity. For a 100 × 70 × 30 mm object, the estimate was 55.45 g instead of 83.30 g; equivalent strength was not demonstrated.

The opening view adds a slider for pressure, initial withdrawal, release, and sliding. Older projects remain compatible and can retain the key-based model. Separate STL and 3MF exports, geometry checks, and material comparisons were added, along with Manifold integration and CMake discovery for Windows builds.

This was a mechanical preview without a physical print. Click force, fit, PLA fatigue, and transport strength still required testing. The opening view does not simulate stress. Check the rear bridge and rails in a slicer, secure shipments with tape, and use the parcel's actual measured weight. Exports contain no printer profile or G-code.
