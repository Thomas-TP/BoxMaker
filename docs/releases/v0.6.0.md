# Boxmaker v0.6.0 — Printable shipping seal

> **Historical release.** The unsigned Windows installer was removed. [Download the current Boxmaker release](https://github.com/Thomas-TP/BoxMaker/releases/tag/v0.8.3). The files attached below are retained for automatic updates of existing installations.

The sliding box can now use a single-use seal printed entirely in PLA. Two hooked anchors join the box and lid. The recipient breaks the central tab before using the normal latch. No purchased component is needed.

The option is enabled in new projects and can be turned off. Older saved projects keep their seal-free geometry until you enable it. The complete 3MF contains three separate objects: box, lid, and seal. You can also export only the seal for a later shipment.

To pack a parcel, print the three parts, inspect slots and bridges in your slicer, load and close the box, and press the two seal heads into place. The recipient lifts and breaks the centre, then presses the latch and slides the lid. The remaining anchors can be removed from inside after opening.

**Experimental mechanical preview.** Digital geometry and exports were checked, but engagement, break force, transport strength, and resistance to removing the seal intact have not been verified on a real print. Test with your PLA, nozzle, and final box size before shipping. A visible break does not guarantee tamper resistance.

`releases.win.json` and the full `.nupkg` are update files, not manual installers. Their hashes are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
