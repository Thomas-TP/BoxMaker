# Boxmaker v0.2.1 — Integrated latch and lighter box

> **Historical release.** The unsigned Windows installer was removed. [Download the current Boxmaker release](https://github.com/Thomas-TP/BoxMaker/releases/tag/v0.8.3). The files attached below are retained for automatic updates of existing installations.

The sliding lid now locks with an integrated rear tab: press the textured area and pull to open it. The previous separate key and its large reinforcement are removed from the new model.

- Two parts, rounded corners, thinner panels, and local ribs.
- An estimated 32–33% less PLA across three formats with the same useful cavity as the standard v0.1 settings. For a 100 × 70 × 30 mm object, the estimate was 55.45 g instead of 83.30 g. Equivalent strength was not demonstrated.
- An opening slider illustrates pressure, initial withdrawal, release, and sliding while preserving the camera angle.
- Older projects remain compatible and can retain the key-based model. Separate STL and 3MF exports remain available.
- Manifold integration and CMake discovery support Windows builds.

Tiny edges produced by geometry operations are reconstructed before export. Tests inspect STL and 3MF coordinates to ensure triangles do not collapse during conversion. v0.2.0 was not distributed.

**Mechanical preview:** No physical print had been made. Click force, fit, PLA fatigue, and transport strength still needed testing. The opening view illustrates motion, not structural stress. Inspect the small rear bridge and rails in a slicer, secure shipments with tape, and use the actual measured parcel weight for postage. Exports contain no printer profile or G-code.

`releases.win.json` and the full `.nupkg` are update files, not manual installers. Their hashes are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
