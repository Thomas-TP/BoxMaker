# Privacy

Boxmaker designs and exports printable boxes locally on your computer. It does not require a Boxmaker account and does not contain ads, usage tracking, or analytics.

Your projects, dimensions, weights, and exported models stay on your device unless you choose to share them. If you open a link to Swiss Post, GitHub, or Microsoft Store, that external service applies its own privacy policy.

On macOS and Windows Velopack installations, Boxmaker checks GitHub for updates at startup by default. You can disable **Check on startup** in Updates, or request a check manually. Downloading and installing updates remains your choice. Microsoft Store installations receive updates through the Store instead. These services may process technical data such as your IP address according to their own policies; project dimensions and models are not included in update requests.

Boxmaker stores your language, update preferences, and last valid project draft locally in the app's webview storage. The draft supports recovery after closing or restarting and is replaced as you edit a valid project. Uninstalling or clearing app data may remove it; save a project file for a durable backup. No draft is uploaded by Boxmaker.

Boxmaker's Swiss Post estimates are calculated in the app from published rates; the app does not send your box dimensions or weight to Swiss Post.
